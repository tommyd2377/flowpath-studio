## Working principles

- Publishers supply knowledge, capabilities and identity. Shared, validated machinery adapts to visitor intent across unrelated businesses and subjects; fixes must not depend on one publisher or product name.
- The regular website must be useful on its own. Voice and chat share one evolving information surface, with the website receding while it is active and returning intact when it closes. Do not reintroduce a detached, duplicate browse/filter application.
- Related information enriches the current state; a new subject may replace it. Back and Forward restore whole answer states without replaying actions. Desktop expansion generally keeps earlier supporting material left and new primary material right; mobile prioritizes useful content in limited space.
- Spend the information card's space on the answer. Keep counts and controls compact, preserve needed qualifications, and use structured records for browsing when they exist. Long source prose remains available for reading.
- Useful alternatives require contextual judgment. The unlabeled gallery was a good response to one picture request, not a universal fallback. Never claim an unverified photo identity or invent missing facts/capabilities.
- Motion should clarify continuity, focus and change. Keep content crisp, respect reduced motion and keyboard access, and preserve media through layout changes. More animation is not itself an acceptance criterion.
- Current intent and manual actions supersede stale work. The assistant must receive the acknowledged visible state, retain relevant constraints, and wait for display confirmation before claiming an action succeeded.
- Conversation memory is independent of presentation history. Retain completed dialogue and applicable researched findings across screen changes, Home, Back, voice/chat and reconnects within the same visit. Keep sources, time and conditions; do not force another search or assume every later item follows the same rule.
- Keep review → Apply to draft → Publish separate. Changes to source files, approved knowledge and media must reconcile through that lifecycle; a draft reset must not silently publish or delete another site's material.

These refine the existing [product philosophy](product-philosophy.md). The September 19 choice for note 13 is **softly fading outer edges with crisp content**, not blurred text or images.
