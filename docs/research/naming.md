# Naming proposals

October 26, 2026. These are working creative directions, not availability research or a rename decision. No domain registrar, trademark register, app-store account or purchase flow was accessed. Domain ownership, prices, availability and legal clearance are unverified. No product copy, links, package names or application identifiers changed.

| Working name | Product connection | Risk to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FlowPath Studio | Preserves the established identity while the creation experience grows. | Whether users understand what they can create without a short explanatory line. |
| Wavefolk | Suggests a collection of distinct voice personalities. | Existing businesses/marks, pronunciation, and whether it feels too character-focused. |
| Voicehaven | Suggests a personal place for voice experiences. | Existing uses and whether it implies privacy guarantees beyond the product. |
| Talkloom | Suggests building or weaving a conversation. | Existing uses, spelling recall and voice-product confusion. |
| Persona Harbor | Suggests a home for creator-authored personas. | Length, existing uses, and implications of real-person impersonation. |

Domain *patterns* to evaluate after a shortlist are `<name>.app`, `<name>.studio` or `get<name>.com`. These placeholders are not claims that any particular address exists or can be acquired. Keep the existing approved domain and stable `/p/<id>` link shape until a separate rename/migration is authorized.

A useful next decision is whether the product should lead with creator-owned **voice experiences**, **characters**, or a broader **app-building studio**. For the current MVP, “Create a voice experience” describes the actual capability without implying websites, arbitrary plugins or unrestricted tools. Keeping FlowPath Studio is the lowest-change option while testing that description; choosing a new name remains open.

After selecting two candidates, the owner can commission proper trademark/domain/app-store checks and test spelling recall with users. Any purchase, registration or branding migration requires a separate explicit instruction. Public name research alone would still not establish legal clearance.
