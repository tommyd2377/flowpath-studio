# Brand assets

The FlowPath PNGs are the existing product assets. Account entry reuses `flowpath-icon-square.png` and the FlowPath Studio wordmark treatment from the settings header.

`google-g.png` is the unmodified Google Identity logo downloaded from https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png on October 27, 2026. Use it at its native aspect ratio in the light Google sign-in button, with its original colors and a visible “Continue with Google” label. See https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines for the official assets and button guidance. It is served locally; no third-party asset request or authentication-provider change is added.
